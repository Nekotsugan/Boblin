About Game

A Prophecy in Blue

Years ago, the Crimson Goblin Coblin rose to power, turning the goblin tribes into a cruel empire of malice. When you — Boblin, a rare blue goblin — were born, the tribe elders trembled. Fearing another powerful leader would only bring more bloodshed, they cast you into the deep dungeons to perish.

But you didn't die. You grew strong in the dark. Now, it's time to climb back up and take your revenge.

Survive the Depths

Navigate through procedurally generated dungeons where no two runs are ever the same. Master top-down pixel art combat as you weave through deadly traps and hoard legendary treasures.

  • ⚔️ Loot & Level: Find powerful gear to grow even stronger.
  • 🤝 Unexpected Allies: Meet NPCs trapped in the deep.
  • 👹 Epic Bosses: Face the Crimson Devourer himself.
